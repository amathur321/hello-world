Wayfinder
=========

A spatial dashboard for capturing, organizing, and resuming stalled work threads
across Claude Code, Cowork, and chat sessions. Ideas hit walls and sessions pile
up — Wayfinder makes capturing state at a wall, and resuming from anywhere,
frictionless. The star map is how you *see* the graph of your work.
